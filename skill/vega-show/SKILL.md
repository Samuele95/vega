# /vega-show — the network, shown

You are the curator of one exhibit, and the rule you never bend is **the page says nothing the chain did not measure, and touches nothing the chain shipped**. Every number the page shows is a fact key: a script extracted it from the bundle's pinned records or from the report's own labels, and the resolver refuses a digit in your prose. The bundle is read, verified and served, never written: the showcase lives in the slot the bundle's service opens, under its own manifest, which pins the bundle it describes and the report it cites. You choose the words, the order and — within closed sets — the lexicon, the motif and the class colours. You do not retrain, re-evaluate, re-pack or re-document, you do not write `story.json`, `page.json` or `SHOWCASE.json` (scripts do), and you do not edit the page template to fit a dataset.

## Where this skill sits

The presentation stage that follows the **network sub-chain** of the Vega package: input `<stem>.pack.html` (read-only; a `/vega-pack` file) and the bundle it names → output `<stem>.pack.show.html` plus `<bundle>/showcase/`. Upstream: `/vega-pack` (the bundle, its pinned records, `serve/` with the showcase slot, `MANIFEST.serve.identity`); `/document-project` in Vega mode when the lane documents (the compiled report, its `.aux` and `docs/label-roles.json`). Downstream: the reader, who opens the served page. `/vega` schedules you after `pack`, and after `document` when the lane documents, and checks that your HTML was published and your brief written. Your brief is **post-report**: it is never a chapter of `/document-project`, and nothing in the report cites the page — the showcase pins the report, never the other way round, so a new report digest makes the showcase stale and `/vega` proposes `show` again.

## Inputs

1. `<model>.pack.html` with the `geometry`, `shape`, `architecture`, `training`, `evaluation` and `bundle` layers (`analysis` optional). The bundle directory is `bundle/<slug>-<run>/` next to the model, from the bundle layer's manifest. The report is optional.
2. Flags: `--bundle <dir>` (override the bundle directory); `--report <docs dir | main.pdf>` (the compiled report; default `docs/label-roles.json` beside the model or in the working directory, else `docs/*.pdf` with a same-stem `.aux`); `--label-roles <json>` (an explicit role map, overriding the one beside the report); `--plan <edited story-plan.json>` (the re-run path for wording: no new facts and no new draft — the edited plan is re-validated, resolved and rebuilt; see *Re-runs*); `--carry <story-plan@1|@2>` (carry the authored text of an earlier plan); `--dataset <name>` (another name the memory's `show:` lines give this dataset); `--python <interpreter>` and `--port <n>` (the interpreter and port the served check starts the bundle's service with; default the current interpreter and a free port); `--unattended` (no user present: every fork takes its unattended answer, below); `--out <path>` (default `<input-stem>.show.html`).

## Dependencies — read, do not duplicate

This skill's own: `references/show-contract.md` (the stage, the fact set, the story plan and its rules V1–V15, the story, the showcase directory and `SHOWCASE.json`, the slot, report binding, the page template, the layer, the brief, the gates) and its machine copy `scripts/catalog.json`; the scripts (`scripts/README.md` lists them and the order); the shipped assets — `assets/page/` (the page template, identified by `TEMPLATE.json`), `assets/showcase-render.js` and `assets/showcase-layer.css` — copied or mounted, never rewritten per run. `vega-pack`: `references/bundle-contract.md` §1, §2 and §7 (the bundle, `MANIFEST.json`, serving, the showcase slot §7.6, inspection §7.7, re-emit §7.8). `vega-forge`: `scripts/catalog.json` (tasks, families, sample shapes, decision families, `metric_direction`, `inspection.links`) and `scripts/backends/<name>/explain-catalog.json`. `vega-eval`: `scripts/catalog.json` (the metric set per task) and `references/eval-contract.md` §5–§6 (the verdict and the readings' fixed forms, quoted, never rewritten). `vega/references/brief-contract.md` (the stage brief). `document-project` (patched, Vega mode): `docs/label-roles.json`. `domain-forge`: `scripts/apply_layer.py` (through `scripts/apply_show_layer.py`), `scripts/validate_model.py`, `scripts/strip_layer.py --list`. Open these when needed; do not restate them.

## Procedure

`OK:` / `WARN:` / `ERROR:` before prose at every step; `FORK:` when you stop to ask — and under `--unattended` no `FORK:` waits: each fork takes the unattended answer the table below states, marked. There is no `GATE:`: nothing in this stage is irreversible, because the previous showcase is kept as `showcase.prev/`. Every script prints its markers and appends them to the showcase's `.work/markers.json`; a refusal names its gate (`ERROR: <gate> <where>: <why>`, exit 2) and is fixed where the gate points, never worked around. Below, `<b>` is the bundle directory and `<s>` is `<b>/showcase.building`, where the steps before the build leave their files.

**M. Read memory.** `.claude/domain-forge-memory.md` § **Network stances**: the lines prefixed `show:` for this dataset (roles with their basis, motif, lexicon nouns, earlier showcases) and the pack line (the ship stance and the exposure). A recorded stance is applied and cited, never re-asked; `draft_plan.py --memory` reads the same lines.

**0. Read and verify.** `strip_layer.py --list` on the model. Resolve the bundle directory and the report. The preflight is the extractor's (step 1), not yours to re-implement, and every one of its refusals stops the stage: the bundle layer's manifest is not `MANIFEST.json` (PF1: the bundle was re-packed after this page); a pinned record differs from its `MANIFEST.files` digest (PF2); `predict.py --bundle <b> --check` fails from a clean shell (PF3: the page must not present a bundle that no longer reproduces); the bundle has no service or no `MANIFEST.serve.showcase_slot` (PF4: `ERROR:` naming `/vega-pack <eval.html> --serve` — not a fork, the lane stops); the label-role map's `report.aux_digest` is not the digest of the report's current `.aux` (`ERROR: PF6 …: the role map is older than the report's .aux — recompile /document-project` — not a fork); a task, family, block type or sample shape the vega-forge catalog does not know, or a task the vega-show catalog has no view key for (PF7: `ERROR:`, never skipped — and the vega-show catalog's sync test, CS1, holds its per-task, per-shape and per-block tables to cover every task kind, sample shape, graph level and block type the vega-forge catalog lists, so a case the forge knows never meets a missing row); a failed round trip (PF8). The build (step 6) holds its inputs to PF1, PF2, PF4, PF6 and PF8 again, re-validates the plan (V1–V15), and adds PF5 (the page's `requires_endpoints` ⊆ `MANIFEST.serve.endpoints`) and PF9 (`MANIFEST.files` pins no `showcase/` path).

**1. Extract the facts.** `python3 scripts/extract_facts.py --model <m> --bundle <b> [--report <r>] [--label-roles <j>] --out <s>/plan/facts.json`. Every number the page can show is here, with the record ref it came from, and `names` is the whitelist of tokens that may carry digits. You never edit this file: the plan pins its digest (V1).

**2. Draft.** `python3 scripts/draft_plan.py --facts <s>/plan/facts.json --page-manifest assets/page/TEMPLATE.json --memory .claude/domain-forge-memory.md [--dataset <name>] [--carry <old plan>] [--unattended] --out <s>/.work/story-plan.draft.json`. The draft proposes the lexicon, the motif, the class roles, the input groups, the choices, the runs with mechanical labels, the results facts, the limits, the context steps and the code-walk steps with their report roles, and prints the forks it left open. With `--carry`, every authored slot the new facts still support is carried and every one it cannot carry is a `WARN:`.

**3. Forks.** At most three, and only those the draft printed as open:

| fork | when | attended | unattended |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 report** | No compiled report was found (`document` did not run). A report without a role map is not this fork: its roles are proposed from the `.aux` and you confirm them in the plan. | `FORK:` continue with **brief links** (each code note links to the stage brief that documents it; the build then takes `--brief-links`), or stop and run `/document-project vega <lane>` first. | Unattended, `build_showcase.py --unattended` continues with brief links and `WARN: no report — code notes link to the stage briefs; re-run /vega-show after /document-project`. |
| **S2 roles** | The draft proposes an `alert` role, or `domain:` / `spec:` evidence raised one, and no `user:` turn or `show:` stance settles it. | `FORK:` per-class roles, the proposal first: neutral X (its structural rule), alert for …, plain for the rest. The answer is `user:<turn>` in `authored.class_roles.source`. | Unattended, `draft_plan.py --unattended` turns an unsupported `alert` into `plain` with `WARN:` naming the downgrade; a structural `neutral` is kept; the anomalous side under `task:anomaly` stays `alert`. |
| **S3 motif** | The modality admits more than one motif and no `show: motif` stance exists. | `FORK:` motif, the modality's base first, each admissible variant with a one-line reason. | Unattended, `draft_plan.py --unattended` applies the modality's base, marked. |

Attended, ask the open forks in one `FORK:` message, S1 then S2 then S3. The S2 and S3 answers become `show:` stance lines (step 13), applied without asking next time; the S1 answer is the build's `--brief-links` flag, not a stance — it holds for this run only, because a report that appears later changes the answer; under `--unattended` nothing is asked and every answer is the table's. An unearned verdict is not a fork, and neither is one that is not assessable: the page shows the verdict as the evaluation recorded it — unearned with `WARN:`, not assessable in words with the evaluation's reason.

**4. Author the story.** Copy the draft to `<s>/plan/story-plan.json` and fill `authored` per contract §3.2 — never `proposals`, never the pins. Read the prose sources the draft names (the geometry, analysis and shape readings, the netspec rationales, the ladder hypotheses, the evaluation's verdict and limits readings, the pipeline's system description, the domain model's comments) and write connected prose, motive before mechanism, for a reader who was not in the chain. Write every number as a `{fact_key}` placeholder (a number no fact holds is not written at all), and the nouns the lexicon keys name — the row, the feature, the source and, per shape, the step, the window, the node, the edge, the graph — through their lexicon paths (`{row.one}`, `{feature.many}`, `{source.the}`), so one lexicon entry renames them everywhere. The page template draws every domain noun from the lexicon, never from a literal; in your prose, the same nouns are the same lexicon paths. Write no digit, no record path, no page or section number. A name that carries digits is allowed only if `facts.names` holds it (the columns, classes, run and block ids, metric and family names, the dataset stem, the service title and the catalog's `name_tokens`); any other such term is described without its digits (never by spelling a number out: a number word is no way round the rule, step 5), or added to `name_tokens` by a reviewed catalog edit — never excused per run. The verdict itself is data, not prose: the resolver writes the results section's verdict (`earned`, `beaten_by_baseline`, the strongest baseline and the difference) from the facts as the evaluation recorded it, and the page renders it; your headline and notes may explain it but keep the evaluation's verdict word as the evaluation states it ("earned", "not earned", "beaten by a baseline", or "not assessable" with the evaluation's reason), never a softer or stronger one. A verdict that is not assessable (eval-contract §5, A30: `earned: null` with a `reason` and no baseline, difference or interval — an unlabelled anomaly task, whose models' scores live on their own scales) is neither a win nor a loss: say *not assessable* and why, in the evaluation's terms, state the network's primary with its interval through its fact key, and name no baseline as beaten or beating. Cover every mandatory limit, give `alert` only on a `user:` turn, a `show:` stance or `task:anomaly` (`registry:<lane>` is the verification harness's basis: V6 admits it only when `VEGA_LANE_REGISTRY`, which the matrix sets and a production run never does, names a lane registry declaring that role; never write it yourself), partition the inputs into the proposed bounds, set `locale` to `en`, and set `status: authored`. When the draft proposed report roles (a compiled report without a role map, marked `WARN: label roles proposed from the .aux titles …`), confirm or correct each in `authored.report_roles` — a label, never a number; V14 holds each to the role's kind in the `.aux`.

**5. Resolve.** `python3 scripts/resolve_story.py --plan <s>/plan/story-plan.json --facts <s>/plan/facts.json --page-manifest assets/page/TEMPLATE.json [--report <r>] --out <s>/story.json`. Every refusal is printed with its rule id (V1–V15): fix the plan and resolve again; never edit `story.json`. A verdict the evaluation records as not assessable (`earned: null` with a `reason`, A30) resolves as recorded: the story's verdict keeps `earned` and `beaten_by_baseline` null and carries the reason, the page says it in words, and the mandatory limit `verdict_not_assessable` must be covered (V9). The resolver refuses only a malformed one — a null `earned` without its reason, or beside a named baseline, a difference or a beaten flag — and that refusal points at the evaluation record; never substitute earned or not earned to get past it. Number words ("fourteen", "half") are `WARN:` and recorded. A quantity is never written in words either: replace every number word that states one with its fact placeholder and resolve again — and when no fact states that quantity, the prose does not state it. The lint also fires on ordinary English that states no quantity ("the one that", "double-check"); those uses stay, and the brief's *Open* lists the WARNs left.

**6. Build and check.** `python3 scripts/build_showcase.py --bundle <b> --model <m> [--report <r>] [--label-roles <j>] [--brief-links | --unattended] --out <b>/showcase`, then `python3 scripts/build_showcase.py --check <b>/showcase [--report <r>]`, which must exit 0 (2 is broken, 3 is stale: re-run, never patch) and writes its result to `<b>/showcase/.work/checks.json` — under `.work/`, never at the showcase root, where an unpinned `.json` is exactly what CK3 and the slot (SL8) refuse. The build copies the page template byte for byte (nothing is substituted), writes `page.json` and the demonstration sample's attribution through the bundle's own inspector (without an inspector: `WARN:` and no attribution), copies the report or the stage briefs, pins everything in `SHOWCASE.json` with the bundle `MANIFEST.json` digest and the report digests, proves the bundle's files unchanged, and swaps `showcase.building/` into `showcase/`, the previous one kept as `showcase.prev/`.

**7. The served check.** `python3 scripts/smoke_show.py --serve <b> --strict [--python …] [--port …]` starts the bundle's own service with the skills off the path and no bytecode written into the bundle, checks that `/v1/showcase` says `verified: true`, walks every view through the page's probe, checks that no placeholder is unresolved, that the alert colour appears exactly when an alert role was authored, and that every report or brief link answers with its pinned digest; it writes `showcase/.smoke/smoke.json` and screenshots. Without a headless browser, or without the packages the bundle's service runs on (`requirements-serve.txt`): `WARN: served page not checked`, the run is non-strict, and the summary says so.

**8. Author the layer reading.** Write `<b>/showcase/.work/showcase-layer.json` (contract §8's layer data: under `.work/`, never at the showcase root, for the same closed-world reason) as `{"from_showcase": "<b>/showcase", "readings": {"abstract": "…"}, "markers": […]}`. The abstract says what the page shows and to whom, in prose without a digit; `markers` carries every `WARN:` line of this run's `showcase/.work/markers.json` and of the served check, verbatim — what the stage could not do or had to default is what a reader must see (in brief-link mode the `WARN: no report` line, which the apply script requires; without a browser, `WARN: served page not checked`). That marker means the walk did not run; a walk that ran and failed is fixed and re-run, never carried as "not checked".

**9. Write the layer.** `python3 scripts/apply_show_layer.py <m> --data <b>/showcase/.work/showcase-layer.json --out <out>`. It splices `SHOWCASE.json`, the story summary, the bindings, the report binding and the checks verbatim, and refuses a layer whose check is not `pass`, whose served smoke neither passed nor carries `WARN: served page not checked`, whose abstract carries a digit, whose `SHOWCASE.json` does not pin the current bundle manifest, or whose `--out` is the input. It reads the served result from `showcase/.smoke/smoke.json`, never from your `markers`: a `smoke.json` that records a failed walk is refused whatever the markers say, and the not-checked `WARN:` is accepted only when the served check did not run. You write no JavaScript and do not append the block by hand.

**10. Verify.** `validate_model.py <out>` (exit 0, invariants 13–16) and `python3 scripts/smoke_show.py <out> --strict` (the *Showcase* tab mounts and says what the record says; non-strict without a browser, and the summary says so).

**11. Publish the artifact.** `<out>` is the product, and the user sees it before reading about it: with validation and the smoke test passed, open the file in the conversation — through the runtime's artifact or file tool when it has one, otherwise by delivering the file so it renders inline — and say in one line that the *Showcase* tab is the new one and what it shows (the story outline, the fact bindings, the report binding, how to open the page). Give the serve command from `MANIFEST.serve.identity`, and deliver `showcase/.smoke/start.png` beside it when it exists. A file that failed validation or the smoke test is not published; it is fixed. There is no summary of an unpublished stage.

**12. Write the stage brief.** `<b>/showcase/docs/show-brief.md` per `vega/references/brief-contract.md`, after the publish and before the summary: the frontmatter with `stage: show`, `skill: vega-show`, `layer: showcase`, `post_report: true`, `report: {status, pdf_digest, aux_digest, roles_digest}` and the records every number resolves to (`SHOWCASE.json`, `plan/facts.json`, `plan/story-plan.json`, `story.json`, `.work/checks.json`, `.smoke/smoke.json`); then the seven sections under the contract's headings, in its order — `## What this stage was for` (who the page is for, what the bundle handed it), `## What was decided` (the class roles with their basis, the motif and why, the lexicon nouns and their source, the input groups, the report mode, each fork with its answer or its unattended default, marked), `## What ran` (the exact commands with their marker lines), `## What came out` (values resolved and placeholders from `SHOWCASE.json.story`, sections, roles bound and missing, the served walk from `smoke.json`, each with its key), `## What the reader should check` (`build_showcase.py --check`, the serve command, the bindings tab), `## Open` (missing roles, number-word WARNs, a proposed rather than emitted role map; "Nothing open." when empty), `## Next` (the hand-off in one line). Nothing the layer does not hold; every number with its key. The slot never serves `docs/`, and `/document-project` skips a brief with `post_report: true`.

**13. Update memory.** § **Network stances**, one line per decision answered in this run (`YYYY-MM-DD | <dataset> | show: roles <class> <role> (<basis>), … (user)`; `… | show: motif <id> (user)`; `… | show: lexicon <key> <one>/<many> (user)`) and one line for the showcase: `YYYY-MM-DD | <dataset> | show: showcase of <slug>-<run>, report <linked|brief-links>, motif <id>, roles <summary>`. An unattended default is not a stance: it is not written as one.

**14. Summary.** In this order: the published artifact (its path, the new tab) and the brief's path; the showcase directory and the serve command with its URL; the story outline in one line per view; the report binding (roles bound and missing, or brief links); the checks (resolve, build check, served walk, validate, smoke — a check not run is named with its reason); memory updated; the hand-off — open the page; `/vega-show <model> --plan <edited plan>` to change the wording; `/document-project vega <lane>` and then `/vega-show` again when in brief-link mode; the re-run condition (a new report, a re-emitted bundle or an edited stage brief makes the showcase stale; a re-pack needs `show` on the new `….pack.html`). Stop.

**Re-runs.** With `--plan <edited plan>`, the wording changes without a new draft: skip steps 1–4, because the edited plan keeps the facts it was drafted from (`<b>/showcase/plan/facts.json`, pinned in the showcase). Run `python3 <b>/predict.py --bundle <b> --check` from a clean shell — no `PYTHONPATH`, the skills off the path, `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1` so nothing is written into the bundle (PF3, the one preflight gate only the extractor runs; a failure stops the stage, as in step 0); resolve the edited plan against those facts — `resolve_story.py --plan <edited plan> --facts <b>/showcase/plan/facts.json --page-manifest assets/page/TEMPLATE.json [--report <r>] --out <s>/story.json` — and build from the same inputs — `build_showcase.py --bundle <b> --model <m> --plan <edited plan> --facts <b>/showcase/plan/facts.json --story <s>/story.json [--report <r>] [--brief-links | --unattended] --out <b>/showcase`. The build holds them to PF1 (the facts and the story were made from this bundle's `MANIFEST.json` and this model page), PF2, PF4, PF6, PF8 and V1–V15 again; carry on from the check of step 6. A refusal there means the bundle or the report moved since the facts were extracted: run the stage whole, with `--carry <edited plan>` in step 2, so the authored text survives on fresh facts.

## Failure modes — do not

- Do not type a number, a record path, a page number or a section number. The page's numbers are fact keys; the report's numbers come from its `.aux` through a role.
- Do not write `story.json`, `page.json`, `SHOWCASE.json` or `plan/facts.json` by hand, and do not edit a built showcase: the resolver, the build and the extractor write them, and a hand edit is exactly what the check and the slot refuse.
- Do not write any file the bundle's `MANIFEST.json` pins, and do not write in the bundle directory outside `showcase/`, `showcase.building/` or `showcase.prev/`. A bundle a showcase altered is a bundle nobody evaluated.
- Do not put code in the showcase: no `.py`, no generated JavaScript. The page template is shipped and copied byte for byte.
- Do not edit the page template, its strings table or the renderer to fit a dataset. A word that is wrong for this dataset is a lexicon entry, never a literal.
- Do not colour a class `alert` without a basis outside yourself (a `user:` turn, a `show:` stance, `task:anomaly`; the harness's `registry:<lane>` is refused outside the matrix). A calm/threat palette on data that has no threat is a claim the chain never made.
- Do not paraphrase the verdict into something stronger or weaker, and do not drop a mandatory limit. The page carries the evaluation's state and its limits: a verdict that is not assessable is said as "not assessable" with the evaluation's reason (A30), never shown as a win or a loss.
- Do not publish a showcase whose `SHOWCASE.json` does not pin the current bundle `MANIFEST.json` and the current report: stale is re-run, not patched.
- Do not make the report depend on the page: no chapter for this stage, and no instruction to `document` to cite the page.
- Do not summarise a stage whose HTML was not published or whose brief was not written.
- Do not write or edit the renderer or the layer block by hand.

## Done when

The output HTML validates (exit 0, 13–16) and passes the smoke test, or the non-strict run is reported; `story.json` resolved from a plan that passed V1–V15; `SHOWCASE.json` pins every served file, the bundle `MANIFEST.json` digest and the report digests (or records brief-link mode with its `WARN:`); `build_showcase.py --check` exits 0; the served check walked every view with zero page errors and zero unresolved placeholders, or it is reported as not run with its reason; the bundle's `MANIFEST.json` and pinned files are unchanged; the layer was published in the conversation and `docs/show-brief.md` is written with `post_report: true` and every number resolving to its record; the forks S1–S3 were asked, or settled by a stance, or took their unattended answers, marked; memory holds the show line; and the summary gives the serve command and the re-run condition.
