/vega drives the chain.

It runs each stage in order, checks every product and keeps the ledger. Attended, it asks at real decisions; unattended, it takes the documented default and marks it.

The data lane.

  • dataset-forgemaps the data’s shape.
  • data-lenskeeps only findings that change a decision.
  • dataset-shaperturns decisions into a traced, verified recipe.

The network.

  • blueprintsets the architecture.
  • vega-forgewrites the spec, every choice cited.
  • vega-trainchanges one thing at a time, each with a hypothesis.
  • vega-evalopens the sealed test once.
  • vega-packships a bundle proven by a round trip.

Telling it.

  • documentwrites the LaTeX report (optional).
  • vega-showbuilds a showcase that says only what was measured.

It searches like a scientist.

A baseline, then one change at a time inside a budget — the winner chosen by a rule fixed in advance.
